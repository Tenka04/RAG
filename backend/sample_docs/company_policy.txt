ACME CORP EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & COMPANY POLICY (2026 EDITION)

1. WORKING HOURS & REMOTE WORK
All Acme Corp full-time employees work standard core hours from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time. Flexible remote work is permitted up to three days per week with manager approval. Employees working remotely must maintain active status on company communication channels during core hours.

2. ANNUAL PAID LEAVE (PTO)
Full-time team members accrue 24 days of paid time off (PTO) annually. Unused PTO may roll over up to a maximum cap of 5 days into the following calendar year. Extended leave requests exceeding two consecutive weeks require at least 30 days advance notice to the People Operations department.

3. HARDWARE & EQUIPMENT ALLOWANCE
Upon joining, each engineering staff member is issued a choice of an M-series laptop or an NVIDIA RTX workstation. Every team member receives an annual technology stipend of $1,200 for home office ergonomics, monitors, and approved software tools.

4. HEALTH AND WELLNESS BENEFITS
Acme Corp provides comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance with 100% employer-covered premiums for individual employees. Additionally, an annual $600 wellness reimbursement covers gym memberships, athletic equipment, or mental health app subscriptions.
